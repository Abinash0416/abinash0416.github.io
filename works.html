Fampic Registry

Full Stack DeveloperSep 2026 – Oct 2026 · Freelance

Serverless work and payment registry for a photo studio. Every phone and computer holds a full copy of the data and syncs directly with the others.

Hard partConflict resolution without a server: Lamport clocks with a device id tie-break and tombstones for deletes, so every device converges to the same state without using wall-clock time.

  • Device discovery. Devices find each other through a group-secret-derived beacon and connect over iroh, with hole punching and a relay as fallback.
  • Sync protocol design. Devices exchange version vectors and sync only the missing events, with live push and peer-to-peer relaying.
  • Offline-first storage. A local event log and version vector on each device, with entries rebuilt from the log and full export and import as the backup.
  • Security. Event payloads encrypted with a shared group data key, per-login Ed25519 signatures on every event, and Argon2-derived password keys.

Magizh Foods

Full Stack DeveloperFeb 2026 – Present · Freelance

Platform for a cloud kitchen with home delivery. Customers order one-time or subscribe to plans, and the kitchen runs everything from an admin portal. Built solo and deployed on Railway.

Hard partWhatsApp-first flows: OTP login, order notifications, and a reminder 2 hours before the skip deadline so customers can skip a meal inside WhatsApp itself.

  • Solo build. Rails 8 API on Solid Queue, Solid Cache and Solid Cable, a React 19 and TypeScript web app and a Flutter mobile app, both with desktop, tablet and phone layouts. UI and UX were designed first as Claude artifacts prototypes.
  • Four user types. Admin, customer, kitchen worker and delivery partner each have their own flow: admin and customer on web and mobile, worker and delivery partner on mobile only. Access is enforced on the backend with CanCanCan, JWT sessions, Rack::Attack throttling and AES-encrypted role names.
  • Orders, payments and wallet design. One-time and subscription orders with a different address per meal. Razorpay with signature-verified webhooks and refunds, a per-minute job that expires unpaid orders, and WhatsApp approve and reject buttons for admins. A cancelled delivery becomes a wallet credit with a validation hash, spent oldest first under row locks, with the balance computed in SQL and never stored.
  • Flexible plans and ordering. Admins create recurring plans (any mix of meal days, shifts and durations, priced per duration and shift) or one-time event plans with specific dates and per-date prices. Customers choose their own days, shifts and duration, and each shift can have its own address. Every plan edit saves a read-only snapshot, so existing orders keep their prices and meals.
  • Coupon and referral systems. Percentage (capped) or flat coupons with per-user, global user and budget limits, and rules for date range, nth subscription, minimum cart, plan and duration. Validation runs in a fixed order with a preview mode, and the coupon row is locked at checkout to stop overspending. Referrals reward on signup or first paid subscription via share link or mobile deep link, with self-referral blocked.
  • Delivery management. A staff board (ongoing, upcoming, past and calendar views) assigns meals to partners in batches inside the shift window. Partners mark delivered or revoke within 60 minutes of assignment, and updates reach every screen live over Solid Cable and FCM push.
  • Leave announcements and adjustment. An announced kitchen leave cancels the pending recurring meals in its range and appends replacement meals to each subscription in one transaction, with an impact preview. Deleting the leave restores them, and it is blocked if one-time meals or another leave fall in the range.
  • Meal skipping and reminders. A skipped meal is added to the end of the subscription or refunded to the wallet. Recurring jobs queue a WhatsApp reminder with a Skip button, stamped per meal so re-runs never double-send.
